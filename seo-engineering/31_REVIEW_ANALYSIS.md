## Review → content gaps

1. **Bus stop and shops right in front** — mentioned by a guest and in the owner's own Holidu text, absent from the site. For families and skiers without a car this answers a real question ("is there a bus / supermarket nearby?"). **Needs the owner's confirmation** (which bus: free ski bus? distance?) before it is added to the location points, the "at a glance" block and property-facts.json.
2. Cleanliness is praised but not stated anywhere — only worth adding as a quote, never as a claim.
